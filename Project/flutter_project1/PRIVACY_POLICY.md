# AutoCompanion Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: January 29, 2026**

## Introduction

Welcome to AutoCompanion ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and web services.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

- **Account Information**: Email address, password (encrypted), and profile details
- **Travel Plans**: Trip itineraries, destinations, dates, and preferences you create
- **Documents**: Vehicle documents (insurance, RC, DL, PUC) you choose to upload
- **Location Data**: Geographic coordinates when using map and navigation features

### Automatically Collected Information

- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
- **Usage Data**: App features used, interactions, and crash reports
- **Location Data**: GPS coordinates when permission is granted

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Provide and maintain our services
- Generate personalized trip recommendations using AI
- Store and organize your travel documents securely
- Provide map navigation and route planning
- Send service notifications and updates
- Improve app functionality and user experience
- Analyze usage patterns and fix bugs
- Comply with legal obligations

## Data Storage and Security

- All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS
- User passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text
- Documents are stored securely in Firebase Cloud Storage
- User data is backed up regularly
- We implement industry-standard security measures

## Data Sharing

We DO NOT sell your personal information. We may share data with:

- **Service Providers**: Third-party services that help us operate the app
- **Legal Requirements**: When required by law or to protect rights and safety
- **Business Transfers**: In case of merger, acquisition, or asset sale

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Access your personal data
- Update or correct your information
- Delete your account and associated data
- Export your data
- Opt-out of analytics
- Withdraw consent for location access

### How to Exercise Your Rights

- **Delete Account**: Settings → Account → Delete Account
- **Export Data**: Settings → Privacy → Export My Data
- **Contact Us**: support@autocompanion.com

## Location Services

- Location access is optional
- Used for map navigation and nearby recommendations
- Can be disabled in device settings at any time
- Location data is not shared with third parties

## Camera and Storage Permissions

- Camera access is used only for document scanning
- Storage access is needed to save documents and maps offline
- Permissions can be revoked in device settings

## Children's Privacy

AutoCompanion is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe we have collected information from a child, contact us immediately.

## International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for international transfers.

## Data Retention

- Account data: Retained until you delete your account
- Trip data: Retained for 2 years of inactivity
- Documents: Retained until you delete them
- Analytics: Anonymized after 14 months

## Cookies and Tracking

We use:
- **Essential cookies**: Required for app functionality
- **Analytics cookies**: To understand usage patterns
- **Preference cookies**: To remember your settings

You can manage cookie preferences in your browser settings.
